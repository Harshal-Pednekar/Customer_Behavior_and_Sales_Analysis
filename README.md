# Customer Behaviour Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **4,500 customer records** to understand purchasing behavior, sales performance, product preferences, customer segmentation, and subscription patterns.

The data was cleaned and explored using **Python**, analyzed using **PostgreSQL and SQL**, and visualized using **Power BI** through two interactive dashboards.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Identify high-performing products and categories
- Understand customer subscription patterns
- Analyze revenue across different customer segments
- Identify purchasing and payment trends
- Generate actionable business insights

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python** – Data Cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis
- **Pandas & NumPy** – Data Manipulation and Analysis
- **PostgreSQL** – Database Management
- **SQL** – Business Analysis and Querying
- **Power BI** – Data Visualization & Dashboard Development

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **4,500 customer records** with information related to:

- Customer ID
- Age & Age Group
- Gender
- Location
- Product & Category
- Purchase Amount
- Unit Price & Units Sold
- Discount Applied
- Payment Method
- Subscription Status
- Review Rating
- Season
- Shipping Type
- Purchase Frequency

A **Customer Segment** column was created in Power BI to categorize customers as:

- New
- Returning
- Loyal

---

## 🔄 Project Workflow

**Raw Data → Python → PostgreSQL → SQL Analysis → Power BI → Business Insights**

### 1. Python
- Cleaned and prepared the dataset
- Handled data inconsistencies
- Performed exploratory data analysis
- Created required derived columns

### 2. PostgreSQL & SQL
- Loaded the cleaned dataset into PostgreSQL
- Performed SQL-based business analysis
- Analyzed revenue, products, customers, discounts, subscriptions, and purchasing patterns

### 3. Power BI
- Connected PostgreSQL database to Power BI
- Created calculated measures and customer segments
- Developed interactive dashboards
- Added KPIs, charts, filters, and business-focused visualizations

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Clothing was one of the highest revenue-generating categories.
- Fall and Spring showed strong revenue performance.
- Credit Card and Debit Card were among the leading payment methods by revenue.
- Subscribers represented a smaller portion of customers compared with non-subscribers.
- A significant portion of customers belonged to the Loyal segment.
- Revenue varied considerably across locations and customer demographics.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Optimize inventory for high-demand products.
- Use cross-selling strategies to improve lower-performing categories.
- Personalize offers based on customer purchasing behavior.
- Strengthen customer retention through targeted engagement.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-potential locations and customer segments.
- Optimize popular payment options for a smoother customer experience.
